tomo-material

Interactive, browser-based teaching demos for computed tomography — from discrete projections through the Radon transform to fan-beam and helical cone-beam scan geometry.

Basic projections

Draw on an 8×8 grid and watch the horizontal and vertical projections update live. The simplest illustration of what a projection is.

Image → sinogram

Paint or upload an image, then build its sinogram one projection angle at a time. Control the number of angles and rotations.

Fan-beam & helical geometry

A 2D fan-beam view synchronised with a 3D helical cone-beam scene: source, detector arc, ray through the selected pixel, and the helical source trajectory.
